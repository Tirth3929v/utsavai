## 🛠️ Recording Tips for Maximum Impact
1. **Resolution:** Record at 1080p (1920x1080) at 60fps or 30fps.
2. **Audio:** Use a decent USB mic or headset; speak with confidence and energy.
3. **Screen Setup:** Close unrelated browser tabs; zoom browser to 100% or 110% so text and charts are crisp and readable.
4. **Tool:** Use [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/), [Loom](https://www.loom.com/), or Windows Game Bar (`Win + Alt + R`).
